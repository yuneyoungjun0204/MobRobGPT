### Image Purpose
학술 논문 서론의 개념도. 다수의 저비용 자폭 무인수상정이 고가치 모선으로 여러 방위에서
수렴하고, 그보다 느린 소수의 방어정이 추격 대신 예상 접근로를 앞질러 포획 그물벽으로
닫는다는 교전 개념을 성립시킨다. 방위산업·해양공학 학술지의 작전 개념도(operational

그 위에 CAD 주석을 오버레이한 2층 구조**다.

### Target Audience
조선해양공학 학술지 심사자와 독자. 캡션 한 줄과 이 도판만으로 "방어측이 더 느린데 왜

### Key Message
느린 소수의 방어정은 적을 쫓지 않고, 적이 지나갈 좁은 접근 회랑을 앞질러 그물벽으로 닫는다.

### Scene Description
A high-altitude near-vertical aerial view of a real coastal sea area, rendered photographically at
the fidelity of a satellite orthophoto or a high-resolution drone survey frame. The water is genuine
open sea seen from above, with visible fine wave texture, subtle swell banding, and a natural depth
gradient from a lighter blue-grey in the shallows along the upper-left margin to a deeper desaturated
blue toward the lower right; it is emphatically not a flat colour fill. A real irregular coastline
with a small rocky headland and its shallow reef shelf enters the upper-left corner, carrying muted
terrain shading and a thin natural surf line where water meets rock. Sun is high and slightly to the
upper left, so every hull casts a short, soft, physically consistent shadow onto the water.

At the geometric centre sits the high-value mothership, a photorealistically rendered grey naval
frigate seen from directly above at roughly one-fifth of the frame height: the hull taper, forecastle,
superstructure blocks, mast, funnel, helicopter deck and deck plating are all legible, and a broad
white wake trails astern with realistic turbulent foam. Ten small suicide craft are distributed around
the outer half of the frame in three distinct loose groups arriving from three separated bearings.
Each is a photorealistically rendered small rigid-hull fast attack boat seen from above, roughly one
twenty-fifth of the frame height, each cutting a sharp white V-shaped bow wake and a narrow foaming
trail that unambiguously indicates its heading and speed. Three defending unmanned surface vessels,
visibly fewer than the attackers and rendered with the same photographic fidelity but with a distinct
lighter hull and a compact deck-mounted net canister aft, are positioned in the mid-field, one facing
each arriving group; their wakes are shorter and narrower than the attackers' wakes, so the speed
disadvantage is legible from the foam alone.

Across the mouths of two of the three approach corridors a capture net barrier is already deployed and
is rendered as a real physical object: a taut line of small orange surface floats strung at regular
intervals across the water with the dark mesh visible just below the surface between them, slightly
disturbed water on either side, and a defending vessel sitting at one end of each barrier having just
laid it. One attacking craft immediately behind one of these barriers is shown dead in the water, its
bow wake collapsed into a flat disturbed patch with no forward foam, fouled in the mesh. The third
corridor is still open, and its defending vessel is under way along a short track toward the position
where its barrier will go, leaving an active wake.

Over this photographic base sits a crisp vector annotation layer, drawn as if a CAD overlay were
placed on the imagery. Two thin white-cored range rings with a fine dark outline are centred exactly on
the mothership, an inner one close around it and a much larger outer one, the annular band between
them carrying a very light translucent blue wash that never obscures the water texture beneath. Three
translucent sector wedges, one per arriving group, open outward from the mothership along each group's
bearing, filled at low opacity in warm red and bounded by thin dashed red edges. From each attacking
craft a thin dashed red track runs inward toward the mothership. From each defending vessel a thin
solid blue track runs to its net position, terminated by a filled triangular arrowhead, and each
deployed barrier is additionally marked by a bold blue overlay segment with small hollow blue circles
at its two endpoints. A thin white dimension line with end ticks spans the outer ring radius. Small
velocity annotation pairs sit beside one attacker track and one defender track, drawn as two short
parallel tick marks whose spacing is visibly wider on the attacker, so the speed relation is carried
by the annotation geometry.

### Rendering Style
- 베이스: **실사**. 위성 정사영상 또는 고고도 항공 촬영 품질의 실제 해면.
  단색 면, 벡터 바다, 만화풍 파도, 플랫 일러스트 금지.
- 선박: **사진 수준 탑다운 렌더**. 갑판 구조물·함교·마스트·항적 거품·선체 그림자가 보인다.
  속 빈 삼각형, 쉐브론, 아이콘, 픽토그램, 실루엣으로 배를 대체하면 **즉시 실패**다.
- 그물: 아이콘이 아니라 **물리적 실체** — 등간격 주황 부표 줄 + 수면 아래 비치는 망.
- 시점: 거의 수직 탑다운. 약간의 기울기는 허용하나 수평선이 보이면 안 된다.
- 조명: 좌상단 고각 태양 하나. 모든 그림자 방향이 일치한다. 렌즈 플레어·글로우 금지.
- 오버레이: 사진 위에 얹은 **CAD 도면**처럼. 반투명 면 + 얇은 실선/파선 + 채워진 삼각 화살촉.
  오버레이가 아래 사진 질감을 완전히 덮지 않는다(불투명도 25 % 이하).
- 흑백 안전성: 적 항적은 파선, 아군 항적은 실선 — 색을 빼도 선종으로 구분된다.
- 여백: 15~25 %. 라벨이 선박 위에 겹치지 않게 지시선으로 빼낸다.
